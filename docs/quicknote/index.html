Small notes, big relief

Everything you need to get a thought out of your head — and nothing in your way.

One shortcut, instantly

Press ⌃⌥N in any app and a fresh note appears with the cursor already in it. Press ⌃⌥H to hide or show all your notes at once.

Stays out of your way

Notes float above your windows and follow you across Spaces and full-screen apps. They’re built so you can type without switching away from what you’re doing.

Looks like a sticky note

Text sizes itself to fit and stays centered. Choose from 7 colors and 4 fonts, including a handwritten one, and resize or drag notes wherever you like.

Reminders that find you

Set a reminder for 10 minutes from now up to tomorrow morning, or pick a custom time. The note pops up on screen when it’s due.

Temporary by design

Let a note delete itself after an hour, a day or a week — ideal for Wi-Fi passwords, room numbers and quick to-dos.

Saved automatically, kept private

Notes are stored only on your Mac and reappear where you left them after a restart. No accounts, no syncing, no tracking.

Every note, easy to find

The All Notes window lets you search, filter by On Desktop, Reminders or Temporary, and bring any note back onto your screen with a click.

Install in three steps

Download

Grab QuickNote.dmg and open it. It mounts like a normal disk image.

Drag to Applications

Drop QuickNote onto the Applications folder shown in the window, then eject the disk.

Launch it

Open QuickNote from Launchpad or Spotlight. It lives in your menu bar — press ⌃⌥N to start a note.

First launch: QuickNote isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS may block it the first time. Right-click the app → Open → Open (or allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security). You only do this once.
