Built the Mac way

Every app follows the same few principles.

Native & fast

Written in Swift with SwiftUI and AppKit. Universal binaries run natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac. No accounts, no analytics, no data sent anywhere.

Made for the menu bar

Always one click away, never in the Dock’s way. Lightweight enough to leave running all day.

Installing takes a minute

Download the DMG

Choose an app above and download its installer.

Drag to Applications

Open the disk image and drop the app onto the Applications folder.

Open it

Launch from Launchpad. First time only: right-click → Open, since the apps aren’t notarized yet.
